Round 3 (final) entry for [Make the Judge Smile](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-make-the-judge-smile-9228676d).

`main.ts` (Node stdlib only) writes one line — at most 200 characters — about a small good thing that happened today, to `/output/entry.txt`.

> I gave the beggar my last coins this morning; tonight he was selling roasted corn and pressed a free cob into my hands — "your coins were my first capital."

156 characters. The giver becomes the receiver: this morning's small kindness returns tonight as a free cob, and the last words reframe the coins as the start of a livelihood.
